Pagefit OneNote → A4

OneNote pages are an endless canvas. When you print or export them, handwriting gets sliced through the middle of a line or cut off at the edge. Pagefit takes the PDF (or screenshots), finds the blank space between your lines, and lays everything out on clean A4 sheets.

Getting your notes out of OneNote

Pagefit can only rearrange what is in the file, so make sure nothing is cut off when you export. A small, complete export is better than a large one with missing edges: Pagefit enlarges it again.

  1. Open the page in OneNote.
  2. Tap Share (or …) and send a copy of the page as a PDF. Save it to Files.
  3. Open Pagefit in Safari, tap Choose your OneNote PDF and pick the file.
  4. Check the sheets, then tap Download PDF and print that file.

Writing missing on the right?

Then OneNote cut it off during export. Zoom out in OneNote until the whole page fits on screen, take a screenshot (top button + volume up) and give Pagefit the screenshot instead. For a long page, take several screenshots that don't overlap; Pagefit joins them in file-name order.

  1. Open the page in OneNote and choose File → Print.
  2. Click Show Details and set Scale to about 60% so the whole width fits. Pagefit enlarges it again.
  3. Open the PDF menu at the bottom and choose Save as PDF.
  4. Drop that PDF onto Pagefit, check the sheets and download.

Screenshots work too

Press ⇧⌘4, drag around the note, then paste it here with ⌘V. Handy for a single page or a board drawing.

  1. Open the notebook at onenote.com in Safari, Chrome or Edge.
  2. Open the page and choose Print from OneNote's menu.
  3. Pick Save as PDF as the printer and reduce the scale if the right edge is cut.
  4. Give the PDF to Pagefit.

Why the cuts happen

OneNote slices the canvas at fixed heights without looking at what is there. Pagefit measures every row of pixels and moves each page break into a gap between lines. If a break still lands on writing, the sheet is flagged and you can move the break with Earlier or Later.
