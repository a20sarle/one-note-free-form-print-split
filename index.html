Getting your notes out of OneNote

Use a PDF when the page fits the export. If writing is cut off on the right, OneNote dropped it while making the PDF, so use screenshots instead: they show exactly what is on screen, at any width.

Screenshots

  1. Open the page and pinch to zoom out until the whole width of your writing is on screen.
  2. Scroll to the top and take a screenshot: top button + volume up.
  3. Scroll down a little less than a screen, so some writing is in both screenshots, and take the next one. Repeat to the end.
  4. In Pagefit choose Screenshots and select them all at once in Photos.

PDF

  1. Tap Share (or …) and send a copy of the page as a PDF. Save it to Files.
  2. In Pagefit choose PDF and pick the file.

Screenshots

  1. Zoom out in OneNote (⌘−) until the full width fits in the window.
  2. Press ⇧⌘3 for the whole screen, scroll down a little less than a screen, and repeat to the end.
  3. In Pagefit choose Screenshots and select them all. A single screenshot can also be pasted with ⌘V.

PDF

  1. Choose File → Print, click Show Details and set Scale to about 60%.
  2. Open the PDF menu and choose Save as PDF, then give it to Pagefit.

Screenshots

  1. Open the page at onenote.com and zoom the browser out until the full width fits.
  2. Take overlapping screenshots from top to bottom and give them to Pagefit.

PDF

  1. Choose Print from OneNote's menu, pick Save as PDF and lower the scale if the right edge is cut.

How Pagefit places page breaks

Pagefit measures every row of pixels and puts each page break in a gap between lines. To move writing further down, click a sheet where you want extra space and choose 2 cm or 4 cm. The space snaps to the nearest gap between lines, and whatever no longer fits moves to the next sheet. Click a space to remove it. With screenshots, Pagefit also finds where they overlap and removes OneNote's toolbar.
